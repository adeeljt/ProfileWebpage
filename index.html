Who I am

About Me

Profiles

Download CV

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Central Punjab (UCP), Lahore, and the founder and head of LahoriBot, a research group working on robotics and applied AI.

My research sits where robotics meets machine learning. I'm especially interested in data-centric AI: the idea that a well-curated, well-labeled dataset often does more for a real-world system than a bigger model. My group builds perception and manipulation systems for robots and the tools to find and fix the data problems that make them fail in practice.

I earned my PhD in Robotics and Machine Learning from [University, Country]. Before joining UCP, I was an Assistant Professor at FAST National University of Computer & Emerging Sciences (FAST-NUCES), Lahore. I've taught thousands of students in classrooms and online, and I care about making AI understandable to people outside computer science. [Add one sentence on awards or recognitions.]

What I work on

Research

My group asks one practical question: what does it take for an intelligent system to keep working when it leaves the lab?

Robot Learning & Manipulation

Teaching robots to grasp and handle everyday objects from a small number of demonstrations. We focus on affordable hardware so the results can be used in local industry.

Data-centric AI

Methods to find mislabeled, missing, or biased data and fix it systematically. Better data often improves accuracy more cheaply than a larger model.

Perception for Field Robots

Computer vision that holds up in dust, glare, and clutter: agriculture, warehouses, and inspection. We build datasets that reflect conditions in South Asia.

AI Education at Scale

Studying how people learn AI online and in class, and designing course material that makes machine learning accessible to non-specialists.

AgriSense: Data-centric Perception for Crop-Monitoring Robots

Ongoing
  • Funding: [Higher Education Commission (HEC), NRPU — Grant No. XXXXX]
  • Role: Principal Investigator
  • Duration: [2026 – 2028]

A low-cost ground robot that monitors crop health in wheat and rice fields. We are building a labeled multi-season dataset from Punjab farms and tools for spotting labeling errors before they reach the model. The goal is to give farmers useful early warnings using equipment they can afford.

Planned outcomes
  • Open field dataset (10k+ labeled images)
  • Open-source robot software stack
  • Policy brief for provincial agriculture department

Ongoing
